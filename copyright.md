## Source texts and third-party data

### Lotus Sutra (法華經)

- Transcription: We manually transcribed the images of the original manuscripts provided by 陈炳应 (Chen, 1985). (陈炳应. 图解本西夏文〈观音经〉译释[J]. 敦煌研究, 1985(5))
- Philological references: Chen (1985).
- Original [Universal Dependency annotations](https://twwspes.github.io/annodoc/saddharmapundarika_sutra/24_tangut): Authorized by the original annotator, Tak Wai Wong (spes@connect.hku.hk). Much appreciation!
- Chinese translation: Publicly circulating edition.
- English translation: [(Chapter 25) 500 Yojanas](https://www.500yojanas.org/lotus-sutra/chapter25/), licensed under CC BY. 

### Avataṃsaka Sūtra (華嚴經)

- Transcription: We manually transcribed the images of the original manuscripts provided by 荒川慎太郎 (Arakawa, 2011). (荒川慎太郎「プリンストン大学所蔵西夏文華厳経巻七十七訳注」『アジア・アフリカ言語文化研究』第81号、東京外国語大学アジア・アフリカ言語文化研究所、2011年、pp. 147–305)
- Philological references: Arakawa (2011).
- Chinese translation: Publicly circulating edition.
- English translation: Bhikshu Dharmamitra, The Flower Adornment 
  Sutra, Kalavinka Press, 2022. Copyright © 2022 Bhikshu Dharmamitra. Authorized partial use by Kalavinka Press. Buddha bless them!
